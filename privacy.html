Privacy policy

How Folio handles information when you browse the shop, join courses, teach, or use Social. Last updated 20 November 2026.

Privacy Terms Cookies

Who we are

Folio is a bookshop and online school. This policy covers the Folio website, customer accounts, tutor dashboards, course learning, marketplace listings, and Social posts.

What we collect

  • Account details — name, email, and password when you create a learner or tutor account.
  • Learning activity — enrollments, lesson progress, class-test results, and resume points so you can continue where you left off.
  • Selling & teaching — book listings, course materials metadata, tutor applications, and payout-related notes you submit.
  • Social — posts and profiles you publish through Folio Social.
  • Preferences — display currency and similar settings stored on your device.
  • Support — messages you send to help@folio.com or through in-product notices.

On this demo deployment, most account and shop data is stored in your browser (local storage / IndexedDB) rather than on Folio servers. Treat it as local to your device unless we tell you otherwise.

How we use information

  • Run the shop, checkout flows, and course enrollment.
  • Personalize currency display and resume your learning.
  • Operate tutor tools, admin notices, and Social content.
  • Keep the service secure and improve the product.
  • Respond when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only when needed to operate Folio (for example, showing tutors who is enrolled in their course), when required by law, or with your direction. Hosting and analytics providers may process technical logs if enabled on the live site.

Retention

Account and learning data stay until you delete them or clear your browser storage. You can remove local Folio data by signing out and clearing site data for this domain.

Your choices

  • Update profile details from your account page.
  • Change display currency anytime in the header.
  • Request help deleting account-related data at help@folio.com.

Children

Folio is aimed at adults and mature learners. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

Contact

Privacy questions: help@folio.com. Also see our Terms of use and Cookies notice.
