Cookies & local storage

How Folio uses cookies and similar technologies on your device. Last updated 20 November 2026.

Privacy Terms Cookies

Overview

Folio uses a small set of cookies and browser storage so the shop, courses, and account features work. We prefer essential storage over marketing trackers.

What we use

Name / type Purpose Duration
Session (local storage) Keeps you signed in across pages Until you sign out or clear site data
Account & shop data Books, courses, enrollments, Social posts (demo store) Until cleared
Display currency Remembers your preferred currency Until changed or cleared
Learning resume Remembers where you left a lesson Until cleared or progress resets
Course video cache (IndexedDB) Stores chapter video blobs for playback on device Until cleared

Essential vs optional

Essential storage is required for sign-in, checkout, teaching tools, and course playback. Without it, Folio cannot function normally.

Optional analytics or advertising cookies are not used by default on this site. If we add them later, we will update this notice and offer a clear choice where required.

Managing storage

  • Sign out from your account to end the active session.
  • Use your browser settings to clear cookies and site data for folio-bookshop.onrender.com (or your local Folio URL).
  • Clearing storage removes local demo data such as cart-related state, session, and cached course videos.

Third parties

Fonts may load from Google Fonts. Hosting (for example Render) may set technical cookies for security or delivery. Those providers have their own policies.
